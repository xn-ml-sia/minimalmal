03 / AI

Three years building generative AI at Mitte Arts Lab. I presented research on bias in AI training data at NeurIPS in 2020 and 2021, then built two things from that work: a Stable Diffusion and LoRA pipeline that automates marketing asset generation, and an internal ComfyUI tool that let non-technical teammates generate high-fidelity UI mockups from a prompt, before the category existed.

The job is still judgment. Tools compound when there are standards for what a good draft looks like and people willing to throw the average one away. Most teams are still prompting by hand; the leap is workflows you can reuse, then agents on the jobs that should run without a meeting.

  • L1Chats. People prompt by hand. Helpful, but nothing compounds.
  • L3Workflows. A task done the same good way every time, with a human reviewing.
  • L5Agents. Whole jobs run end to end, while people stay on the calls that matter.

Hands-on enough to know where generative models fall short, and where they're already good enough to ship.

The tools are everywhere. The judgment isn’t.

AI is a new layer of expression, not a replacement for taste. I use it to move faster on infrastructure and prototypes — then keep the last ten percent: the call about what ships.
